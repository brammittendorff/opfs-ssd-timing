FROST PoC - SSD contention from JavaScript via OPFS

Times random 4 kB reads on a large OPFS file. When something else touches the same SSD, read latency spikes. Local research use only.

What to try: after calibration finishes and the chart settles, launch a heavy app or open a site in another window - watch the spikes appear. Then leave it idle and watch them fade.
Mitigation (from the paper): capping OPFS to ~1 GB makes the file fit in RAM, so reads are served from the page cache and the channel disappears. Requires crossOriginIsolated for high-res timers (set via COOP/COEP in serve.py).
Firefox: OPFS is capped at ~10 GB, so the read channel only works if that exceeds your free RAM. On high-RAM machines pick the write-flush channel, which times flush() (forced SSD writeback) and needs no large file.
